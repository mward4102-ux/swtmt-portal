Final Project Report for Phase-I, prepared by Prof. Shengqian Ma, University of North
Texas, for PlastiBioFuel LLC. Report date August 7, 2026. Period of performance
January 1 to June 30, 2026, matching the SRA1016 term that ended 07/01/2026.

The document carries a confidentiality mark naming PlastiBioFuel as the sole intended
recipient. It is committed here at Michael Ward's direction, as the recipient. It is not
reachable from the deployed site: Netlify publishes `.next`, built from `src/`, and no
site code references anything under `PlastiBioFuel/`.

Every experimental claim in this run's pitch traces to this file. The split between what
it establishes and what it does not is recorded in `../../FUNDING_REGISTRY.md`, which is
the thing to read first on a later run.
