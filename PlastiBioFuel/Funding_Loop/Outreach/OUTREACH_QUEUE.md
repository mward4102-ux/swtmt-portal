## Standing rules

1. **One outreach per run**, in addition to the run's build, not instead of it. Both batches
   below were written at once because the queue was finishable in one pass and leaving it
   half-verified made work for Michael.
2. **Never write the same office twice** without a reason recorded here.
3. **Verify the channel before writing**, and record the date. If an office publishes no
   address, write for the channel it does publish: a web form, or a call script. Do not guess
   at an address format, and do not defer the target for that reason.
4. **Ask for one small thing** an office can grant without a decision: a notification list, a
   name, or fifteen minutes. Never money, never a set-aside, never preferential treatment.
5. **Never ask about a pending proposal**, and never imply an introduction should bear on a
   review.
6. **Claim only what the record establishes.** The standing constraints in
   `FUNDING_REGISTRY.md` govern outreach exactly as they govern applications.
7. **A referral becomes a queue entry.** When a reply names a person, that name enters this
   queue with the date and the office that referred them. Referrals are the point.
8. **When outreach surfaces a funding opportunity, it goes in the registry**, not here. Two
   did on 2026-08-25: ORNL Innovation Crossroads and the LEEP family.
