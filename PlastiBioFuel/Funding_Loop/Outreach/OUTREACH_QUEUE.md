# PlastiBioFuel Outreach Queue

Strategic outreach to federal offices that fund or steer R&D reachable by an SDVOSB.
Read this file on every run, immediately after `FUNDING_REGISTRY.md`. It is the
authoritative record of who has been written to, when, and what came back.

**Cursor: position 1.** The next run sends the next unsent queue entry and advances it.

## Standing rules

1. **One outreach per run**, in addition to the run's build, not instead of it. Batch 01
   shipped three at once because they are the front doors and they do not depend on each
   other.
2. **Never write the same office twice** without a reason recorded here. A second cold
   letter to a federal small business office costs more goodwill than it buys.
3. **Verify the channel before writing.** Read the address off the office's own page and
   record the date. If no channel is published, the entry stays `anticipated` and the run
   verifies it rather than writing to an address that was inferred.
4. **Ask for one small thing** an office can grant without a decision: a notification
   list, a name, or fifteen minutes. Never money, never a set-aside, never preferential
   treatment.
5. **Never ask about a pending proposal**, and never imply that an introduction should
   bear on a review. Say plainly that nothing is being asked on anything pending.
6. **Claim only what the record establishes.** The standing constraints in
   `FUNDING_REGISTRY.md` govern outreach exactly as they govern applications: no reactor
   is built, no ethanol has been produced, ARG Petro is discussions and not an executed
   agreement, and fuel credit eligibility stays hedged.
7. **SDVOSB is certified through SBA's Veteran Small Business Certification program**, and
   it appears in SAM.gov. Write it that way. An OSDBU officer reads the difference.
8. **A referral becomes a queue entry.** When a reply names a person, that name enters
   this queue with the date and the office that referred them. Referrals are the point.
